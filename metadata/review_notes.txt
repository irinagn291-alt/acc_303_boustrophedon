We appreciate the review. The build under review was retested on a clean iPhone and on an iPad in portrait. Nothing we tried produced a crash, a dead end, an empty screen, or a non-functioning feature.

A few behaviours are deliberate and can look like a stall on a fast pass. Please try these first.

1. Welcome will not dismiss until it is finished (no skip, no swipe; page dots are not tappable). Advance only with "Next" on "A line for captions.", "Next" on "Face each backward word.", then "Continue" on "Keep the faced line."

2. "Face" stays disabled until the status is "Turned", at least one backward word remains, and "Help" is "Guided". In "Blind spot", "Face" stays off so the backward words have to be found by reading. Word chips do nothing while the line is not "Turned". "That line is too short." appears if "Maker", "Title", or "Inscription" has fewer than two words.

3. After every turnable saved painting is faced, home reads "Every saved painting is faced." Use "Practice again", or open "Saved" and choose "Practice maker", "Practice title", or "Practice inscription". "Reset all data" can return home to "Crate level." / "Save a work, then face."
