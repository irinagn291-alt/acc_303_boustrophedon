Version 1.0 introduces Boustrophedon, a quiet practice app for facing National Gallery painting captions.

• Three-step welcome: "A line for captions.", "Face each backward word.", and "Keep the faced line."
• Home screen "Face the words" with "Explore", "Saved", and "Settings"
• Explore by "Maker or title", with a local shelf of National Gallery works
• Save paintings to a crate; lay maker or title lines; face backward words; record yaws
• "Face", "New line", and "Retract"
• "Recently faced" strip; Saved sections "Faced" and "Yaws"
• Settings sources, "Show the welcome again", and "Reset all data"
• Light appearance, portrait layout; data stays on this device
