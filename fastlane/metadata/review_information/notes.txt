Three behaviours are deliberate and can look like a stall on a fast pass. Please try these first.

1. Welcome will not dismiss until it is finished (no skip, no swipe; page dots are not tappable). Advance only with "Next" on "A line for captions.", "Next" on "Face each backward word.", then "Continue" on "Keep the faced line."

2. "Face" stays disabled until the status is "Turned" and at least one "Backward" word remains. Word chips do nothing while the line is not "Turned". After the first save a line is usually already turned; otherwise tap "New line".

3. After every turnable saved painting is faced, status can read "Level" and home can return to "Crate level." / "Save a work, then face." Open "Explore" and save another work to continue.
